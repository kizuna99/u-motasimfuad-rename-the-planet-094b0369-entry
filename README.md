One line renaming a planet of our solar system, with the why, written
to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.py` (pure Python standard library, no
network, no input). The text is 1–200 characters, UTF-8, as the gate
requires.

The rename: Venus becomes "The Pressure Cooker" — 464 degrees, acid
rain, and a whole planet spinning backwards like it's trying to
unscrew itself from the solar system. Every part of the why belongs
to Venus alone.
